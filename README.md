# RPSLK_Game
🎮 Rock, Paper, Scissors, Lizard, Spock
An implementation of the classic expanded game popularized by The Big Bang Theory. Perfect for settling disputes when traditional Rock, Paper, Scissors just isn't complex enough!

📜 Quick Rules Reference
Scissors cuts Paper & decapitates Lizard

Paper covers Rock & disproves Spock

Rock crushes Lizard & crushes Scissors

Lizard poisons Spock & eats Paper

Spock smashes Scissors & vaporizes Rock

## 🕹️ How to Play
1. Run the script and wait for the countdown prompt.
2. Enter your choice when prompted: **rock**, **paper**, **scissors**, **lizard**, or **spock**.
   > ⚠️ **Important Note:** Make sure to type your choice in **lowercase letters** and spell out the **full word** (e.g., `rock` instead of `R` or `Rock`). Any typos or uppercase letters will result in a **DRAW**!
3. View the computer's randomized choice and see who wins the round!
